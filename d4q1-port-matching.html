Answer Key & Explanation
Exam Tip: Port numbers below 1024 are "well-known" ports (IANA registered). Security+ frequently tests whether candidates know which ports are encrypted vs. cleartext and which services share similar port ranges (e.g., LDAP 389 vs. LDAPS 636, HTTP 80 vs. HTTPS 443, Telnet 23 vs. SSH 22). Remember: 53 = DNS, 67/68 = DHCP, 110/995 = POP3, 143/993 = IMAP.
